## The customer

The customer is a wholly owned finance subsidiary of an international automotive group and a major automotive finance provider in China. It supports dealers and individual customers across new and used vehicle finance, leasing, and insurance. Its name is not disclosed in the source.

## The challenge: replacing an aging OCR platform without disrupting lending

The company's existing OCR platform had reached the end of its contract and was no longer receiving technical updates. Recognition accuracy and operational stability no longer matched the needs of a growing lending business.

Errors in vehicle invoices, mortgage agreements, and bank statements created additional review work and potential risk. The replacement therefore had to provide reliable structured data before the company could automate downstream processes.

The new platform needed to handle 27 document categories and support eight core workflows, including pre-loan approval, mortgage registration, loan disbursement, and contract management. Selecting the underlying recognition engine was a critical part of that rebuild.

## The solution: a common recognition portal powered by DocFlow

Following proof-of-concept testing and several rounds of evaluation, the company selected INTSIG DocFlow as the core engine for the replacement platform.

### Bring recognition, testing, and monitoring together

DocFlow powers a unified portal for automated recognition and extraction across all 27 document categories. The portal also provides office document recognition services for employees and dashboards for monitoring business processes.

![The document recognition portal shown in the original case](/images/cases/20260209172616177/figure-1.webp)

### Supply the fields that core workflows need

The service integrates with the company's business applications. It parses vehicle purchase invoices, mortgage agreements, identity documents, and other application materials, then returns the fields required by approval and disbursement workflows.

The eight business scenarios share the same underlying document capability. Review teams spend less time entering and checking fields, while the business systems continue to control approvals and lending decisions.

![An invoice and its structured extraction output in the original interface](/images/cases/20260209172616177/figure-2.webp)

## Reported outcomes

| Scope | Published implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Document coverage | 27 categories |
| Core business coverage | Eight workflows |
| Platform functions | Recognition, testing, monitoring, and employee document services |
| Core integrations | Pre-loan approval, mortgage registration, disbursement, and contract management |

The source reports reduced manual handling and a stable transition from the old platform to the new service. It does not publish a quantified processing-time reduction or an overall accuracy percentage, so those figures are not inferred here.

The result is a maintained document processing foundation that supports the company's current finance operations and provides a path for future workflow expansion.
