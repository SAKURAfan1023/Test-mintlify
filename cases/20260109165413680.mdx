## The customer

![Webasto customer logo](/images/cases/20260109165413680/customer-logo.webp)

Webasto supplies automotive roof and thermal systems. As part of its Asia-Pacific integration strategy, the group brought previously separate operations in markets including Japan, South Korea, India, and Thailand into a shared services center in Shanghai.

The accounts payable team needed to standardize supplier invoice processing across the region and connect the resulting data with SAP.

## The challenge: multilingual invoices at a regional scale

The center processes Chinese VAT invoices and commercial invoices from Japanese and South Korean suppliers. Its previous system struggled with Japanese and Korean text, unusual formats, and handwriting.

That left the team relying on language specialists to enter and check information manually. As the regional business moved into the center, the team also needed to absorb month-end and quarter-end peaks without expanding at the same rate.

Chinese invoice processing had to continue improving alongside the new international workflow, including the required tax compliance checks.

## The solution: multilingual extraction connected to Tangro and SAP

Building on an existing domestic invoice automation project, Webasto selected INTSIG DocFlow for the wider Asia-Pacific workflow.

### Recognize local languages and difficult invoice fields

The implementation is optimized for Japanese and Korean fonts, layouts, and handwriting conventions. It extracts structured information from supplier invoices, including complex remarks and handwritten content, reducing dependence on manual language-specific entry.

![A Korean invoice and extracted fields from the original customer case](/images/cases/20260109165413680/figure-1.webp)

### Handle daily processing and closing peaks

Concurrent processing supports the center's regular workload of dozens of overseas invoices per day and peaks of approximately 100 per day described in the source.

That capacity helped the existing team take on the Japanese and South Korean invoice workflows during the regional integration.

### Carry invoice data into the financial process

DocFlow integrates with Webasto's Tangro and SAP systems. The workflow captures invoices, extracts multilingual information, performs the configured Chinese tax compliance checks, and prepares voucher data for SAP.

The connected systems support the broader accounts payable process from invoice intake through downstream payment operations.

![A Chinese VAT invoice and its extraction results in the original interface](/images/cases/20260109165413680/figure-2.webp)

## Reported results

| Measure | Published result |
| --- | --- |
| Average processing time per supplier invoice | Reduced from five minutes to approximately 15 seconds |
| Reported processing efficiency improvement | More than 95% |
| Japanese and South Korean invoice operations | Brought into the automated process without hiring language-specific entry staff |
| Overseas invoice volume | Dozens daily, with peaks of approximately 100 per day |
| Chinese invoice tax compliance check pass rate | More than 99% |

The compliance pass rate describes the reported check outcome, rather than an OCR accuracy measure. The time figures describe this deployment's invoice workflow, including difficult remarks and handwriting.

The shared services center can now handle more of its regional accounts payable workload through one connected process, reducing repetitive entry while retaining its existing financial systems.

*Results are reported in the original customer case and reflect that implementation at publication.*
