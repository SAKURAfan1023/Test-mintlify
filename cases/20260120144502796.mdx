## The customer

![SPDB Financial Leasing customer logo](/images/cases/20260120144502796/customer-logo.webp)

SPDB Financial Leasing is a non-bank financial institution established with Shanghai Pudong Development Bank as its principal founding shareholder. It provides financial leasing services in areas including aviation, shipping, and infrastructure.

As its business expanded, the volume of purchase, sale, and lease contracts grew to approximately 200,000 a year.

## The challenge: contract volume was growing faster than manual capacity

Staff reviewed contracts, located the required information, and entered it into business systems. That process created three related constraints:

- **Capacity:** a large and growing contract workload exceeded what the existing team could handle efficiently.
- **Data quality:** missing or incorrect clauses, amounts, or dates could introduce downstream financial, legal, and operational risk.
- **Operating cost:** adding people for each increase in volume would raise the cost of serving the business.

The technology and business teams needed a way to extract contract data reliably and return it to core applications at scale.

## The solution: structured contract extraction inside the operating workflow

The company selected INTSIG DocFlow after evaluating the available options.

### Extract the fields that leasing operations depend on

The implementation is tailored to complex contract layouts, specialized leasing terms, and important fields such as leased assets, amounts, durations, rights, and obligations.

DocFlow turns that content into structured data in batches, replacing much of the routine manual reading and entry work.

![A contract and its extracted fields in the original DocFlow interface](/images/cases/20260120144502796/figure-1.webp)

### Connect uploads and results to core systems

APIs integrate the service with the company's internal business applications. Contracts are uploaded, parsed, and extracted, and results flow back to the relevant systems.

The platform supports batches of hundreds or thousands of contracts per day in the described operating environment, helping maintain continuous processing as demand grows.

## Reported results

| Measure | Published result |
| --- | --- |
| Annual contract workload described in the case | Approximately 200,000 |
| Cumulative system calls at publication | More than 1.45 million |
| Back-office staffing during business growth | Remained broadly stable |

The source reports shorter contract processing cycles, less manual entry, and more standardized extracted data. It does not publish a numerical accuracy rate or time-saving percentage for this deployment.

For SPDB Financial Leasing, the operational benefit is the ability to support more contract activity without increasing manual handling at the same pace, while providing more consistent data for management and risk controls.

*Results are reported in the original customer case and reflect that implementation at publication.*
