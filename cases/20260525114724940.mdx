## The customer

A multinational AIoT and security solutions manufacturer serves more than 180 countries and regions. Its technology portfolio combines video, connected sensing, artificial intelligence, and data platforms. The source does not disclose the company's name.

Across the group, document recognition requirements had expanded beyond identity documents to certification records, component specifications, and regulatory standards.

## The challenge: each department was solving the same document problem

Product certification teams manually opened files stored locally or on shared drives, located certificate numbers, model identifiers, and dates, and copied those fields into the product lifecycle management system. A certificate could take more than ten minutes to enter. Component certification staff handled hundreds each month.

![A technical document from the original customer case](/images/cases/20260525114724940/figure-1.webp)

HR faced a different version of the same problem. Reviewers opened leave applications and supporting medical documents, extracted leave types, dates, and hospital details, and compared them with employees' submitted forms. The team handled hundreds of applications daily, with varying numbers of attachments.

The international regulatory team needed to centralize product market-access standards. Its platform, started in 2025, required OCR, structured extraction, translation, clause separation, and interpretation across Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Arabic, and other languages. Maintaining an in-house parsing solution was costly and did not deliver the required accuracy.

## The solution: a shared parsing service, embedded in existing workflows

Following proof-of-concept testing, the manufacturer selected TextIn to build an enterprise document parsing platform.

![The original platform architecture connecting document services to business systems](/images/cases/20260525114724940/figure-2.webp)

### Product and component certification

Uploaded PDF and scanned certificates are parsed into structured fields, including certificate numbers, models, dates, and validity periods. Results populate the appropriate system forms automatically.

Staff move from opening and entering every certificate to checking samples and resolving exceptions. The same approach supports both product certification in the PLM system and component certification workflows.

![Structured extraction from a technical document in the original interface](/images/cases/20260525114724940/figure-3.webp)

### HR leave review

The system extracts key fields from supporting documents and compares them with the employee's application. It flags differences for a reviewer to examine.

Documents with sufficiently consistent formats and fields enter the automated extraction workflow. Other categories continue through manual review. HR staff remain responsible for the final review decision.

### International regulatory research

TextIn parses regulatory documents into structured content. The customer's own platform then performs translation, clause separation, and compliance interpretation.

The deployment supports Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Russian, and Arabic documents, helping teams find the market-access requirements relevant to a product.

![An international regulatory document from the original case](/images/cases/20260525114724940/figure-4.webp)

![The same regulatory document parsed into structured content](/images/cases/20260525114724940/figure-5.webp)

## Reported results after one month

| Measure | Published result |
| --- | --- |
| Recognition of Chinese, English, and tables | More than 99% in the reported evaluation |
| Structured parsing of valid samples | More than 98% recognition accuracy |
| Product certificate processing time | Reduced from more than ten minutes to 2–3 minutes |
| Time reduction for certificate entry | More than 70% |
| Parsing API calls | More than 300,000 in the first month |
| Connected business systems | More than 20 |

The unified gateway gives different departments a standard way to call document services. Certification and HR teams spend less time entering data, while the regulatory platform gains structured multilingual inputs for its own analysis.

*Results are reported in the original customer case and reflect its samples and deployment at publication.*
